# HR Assistant — Guide for Members & HODs

Welcome. This short guide shows how to use the HR Assistant bot on Discord.  
No technical knowledge needed — just follow the steps.

---

## 1. First step: complete onboarding

Before you can use tickets or leave, you must create your profile.

1. Go to the **#onboarding** channel.
2. Click **Start Onboarding**.
3. Fill **Step 1**: Full Name, Contact Number, CNIC → Continue.
4. Fill **Step 2**: Email, Date of Birth (`YYYY-MM-DD`), Address.
5. Choose your **department**: BI, CS, Sales, Marketing, or HR.
6. If you are BI, CS, or Marketing, also choose your **level**:
   - **Member** — if you are a team member  
   - **HOD** — only if you are Head of Department  

### Important: pick the right role

Your Discord role comes from what you select here.

| What you choose | Role you get |
|-----------------|--------------|
| BI + Member | BI Member |
| BI + HOD | BI HOD |
| CS + Member | CS Member |
| CS + HOD | CS HOD |
| Marketing + Member | Marketing Member |
| Marketing + HOD | Marketing HOD |
| Sales | Sales Member |
| HR | HR |

If you pick the wrong department or level, your permissions will be wrong (for example leave may not work, or you may see the wrong channels).  
Choose carefully. If you make a mistake, tell HR so they can fix your profile.

After onboarding finishes, the onboarding channel usually hides for you. You are ready to use the bot.

---

## 2. Open a ticket

1. Go to **#open-ticket**.
2. Click **Open ticket**.
3. The bot creates a private ticket channel just for you (and HR if needed).

Tips:
- You normally have **one open ticket**. Opening again reuses it.
- When you are done, click **Close ticket** (or use `/close`).

---

## 3. Ask a question (policies & handbook)

Inside your ticket, type a normal question, for example:

- What is the leave policy?
- How many annual leaves do I get?
- What is the notice period?
- Can I work from home?

The bot answers from company documents.  
If you need a person: type something like **I need to talk to HR**.

---

## 4. Check your leave balance

In your ticket, ask in plain words, for example:

- What is my leave balance?
- How many leaves do I have left?
- Meri kitni chutti baqi hain?

You can also try **`/myleave`**.

**Note:** If your role is **CS Member** or **Sales Member**, leave balance and leave apply are not available. Please contact HR.

---

## 5. Apply for leave

### Who can apply?

- **BI Member / BI HOD** — yes  
- **Marketing Member / Marketing HOD** — yes  
- **CS HOD** — yes  
- **CS Member** and **Sales Member** — no (contact HR)

### How to apply

1. Open your ticket.
2. Say you want leave, for example:
   - I want to apply for sick leave tomorrow  
   - Apply for leave  
   - Mujhe leave chahiye  
3. The bot shows a leave form.
4. Choose leave type from the dropdown:
   - **Annual Leave**
   - **Sick Leave**
   - **Casual Leave**
5. Click **Fill form** and enter:
   - From date  
   - To date  
   - Half day? (optional — first or second half, same day only; leave blank for full day)  
   - Reason (you can also attach a file if needed)
6. Review the card, then click **Submit to HR**.
7. Wait for HR to approve or reject.

You can only have **one pending leave** at a time.

If you are in BI, CS, or Marketing, your HOD may also get a **notification** of your request. Final decision is still with **HR**.

---

## 6. Withdraw a leave (still waiting)

If your leave is still pending (not approved yet):

- Click **Withdraw** on the leave card, **or**
- Type **withdraw** in the ticket

Only you can withdraw your own pending request.

---

## 7. Cancel an approved leave

If leave was already approved and you need to cancel it:

1. In your ticket say something like:
   - Cancel my leave  
   - I want to cancel my leave  
   - Chutti cancel karo  
2. Pick the leave from the list.
3. Click **Fill form**, give a reason, then **Submit**.

Cancelled days go back to your balance.

---

## 8. Extra notes for HODs

If you are a **HOD**, you may see department channels such as:

- **#bi-hod**
- **#cs-hod**
- **#marketing-hod**

These show leave requests for your team so you stay informed.  
You **cannot approve or reject** leave there. Only **HR** does that in **#leave-requests**.

Your own leave works the same way as for members (apply, balance, withdraw, cancel).

## Quick checklist

| I want to… | Do this |
|------------|---------|
| Join / create profile | **#onboarding** → Start Onboarding → pick correct department & level |
| Ask HR / policy | **#open-ticket** → Open ticket → type your question |
| See leave left | Ask in ticket, or `/myleave` |
| Apply leave | Ask in ticket → Fill form → Submit to HR |
| Undo pending leave | Click **Withdraw** or type withdraw |
| Cancel approved leave | Ask to cancel → Fill form → Submit |
